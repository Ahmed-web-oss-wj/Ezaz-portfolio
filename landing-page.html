Our Story

Started with one question: why does coffee have to be rushed?

Amber & Oak began as a single roasting machine in a garage and a belief that good coffee deserves patience — in the roasting, the brewing, and the drinking. Every cup here is made to order, one at a time, by someone who actually cares how it tastes.
